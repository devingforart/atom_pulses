# Club foreground/support experiment

Starting point: completed `cf42e0f6-252b-4ab0-bc25-f98d9b730d69`.

The general Club ensemble-scene planner and the MIDI window writer now share
the same foreground/support brief. It asks the AI to develop purposeful
secondary material during a lead absence, preserve intentional breathing,
let the chord pulse yield through AI-chosen voicing/articulation/dynamics or
omissions, and prepare the terminal melodic arrival through a legible handoff.

This is a compositional instruction experiment, not a note-processing change.
No prescribed density, mandatory fill, generated note, mute schedule, or extra
track quota is introduced. Existing scene fields hold the decisions. Tests
check that planning and writing both receive the instruction. They do not
establish musical improvement: that requires the generated MIDI and listening.

The comparison is a fresh work with the same request/settings and a new seed,
not a controlled same-MIDI A/B. Local only; Cloud remains unchanged.
