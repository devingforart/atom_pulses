identity owners, actual accent offsets, supporting clock/reinforcement/counterweight/

held notes may ring. A quiet clock can remain outside those named owners.

not restart the hierarchy. Historical plans without it remain unchanged.

cycle phase, half-open boundaries, a continuing quiet clock, conflict rejection
and legacy behavior. These are consistency checks, not proof of musical quality;
the listening trial must still demonstrate a recognizable motif and intensity.
