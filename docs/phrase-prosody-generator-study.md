# General generator experiment: AI phrase prosody

This changes the experimental GENERATOR, not just a selected exported MIDI.
Production's accepted `ai-only-v2-connected-discourse` profile and its binaries
remain unchanged. No commit, promotion, VST install or Cloud deployment is implied.

## Hypothesis and diagnosed gap

The listener identified a clumsy clarinet/bassoon entrance at bars 17.3–19 of
*The Fourth That Returns*. Exact MIDI inspection found that the foreground
checkpoint already contained the clarinet's offbeat attacks; accompaniment did
not introduce a timing corruption. The moving bass and inner pulse were composed
later. The stored phrase plan had no `ensemble_spans`, so the earlier ensemble
planning experiment was not present in that work's actual plan.

The issue is not universally incorrect sixteenths, short notes or independent
rhythms: the listener explicitly likes the complexity of bar 53. Technical grid
validity cannot establish musical organization or human phrasing.

## General architectural change

New opt-in flag: `PULSO_AI_PHRASE_PROSODY_EXPERIMENT`.

1. The AI phrase planner now supplies ensemble spans with `joint_voice_ids`,
   `rhythmic_identity`, `articulation_intent`, and sparse `metric_supports`.
   Instrument identities come from the generated cast, never names or roles
   hardcoded for the diagnostic work. Support times are span-local, not note quotas.
2. `AiCompleteDialogue::owners` uses those AI-selected IDs to include pitched
   rhythmic partners in the joint writing phase, in addition to the protagonist
   and answering voices. A moving bass or inner voice can therefore be authored
   with the melody it interacts with, rather than being added only afterward.
3. The note writer receives the structured relationships and authors every real
   note, accent, sounding duration and rest. Planned supports do not emit notes,
   require matching attacks or automatically shift the generated notes.
4. Remaining accompaniment is composed against the accepted real joint material.
   The existing technical checkpoint and direct-AI rendering paths remain in use.

The joint payload bound is 24 bars in this opt-in workflow versus the existing
48-bar foreground bound; these are serialization limits, not musical phrases,
mandatory arrivals or cadences. Actual earlier accepted notes and the full plan
provide continuity across payloads. The previous experimental short-cast route's
12-owner total-cast limit remains an inherited limitation of this lab, not a new
musical rule introduced by prosody. Larger-cast promotion is not established here.

No new quantitative musical rejection gate, algorithmic quantization, random
humanization, note thinning, pitch correction or rhythm generation is added.
The new schema validates only representation and known cast identities. Legacy
plans remain readable and the disabled flag preserves the prior ownership path.

## Isolation and testing

- Dedicated binary: `build-prosody/Release/pulso_cloud_worker.exe`.
- Local launcher: `web/local-studio/start-phrase-prosody.mjs`, default port 4200.
- Experimental flags are forwarded only in explicit local experimental mode.
  The launcher clears inherited song-specific narrative feedback and the previous
  commissioned rehearsal flag. No work-specific repair prompt accompanies fresh
  generation.
- C++ tests cover disabled schema isolation, structured context, AI-selected bass
  ownership, unavailable-owner exclusion, meaningful syncopation representation,
  support bounds, and absence of automatic MIDI insertion. Pipeline tests pass.
- Node parity tests verify that the flag cannot enter normal local/Cloud defaults.

The exact binary hash and AI flags for each request are recorded in the existing
`experiment-context.json` artifact.

## Iteration results and follow-up architecture

The first two trial IDs above stopped before MIDI writing: the planner mixed
phrase-local and span-local `arrival_beat` coordinates. The new protocol now uses
an explicit span-relative `arrival_offset`; legacy plans retain their existing
meaning. Context conversion adds that offset to the span start, not to performed
notes. A nonzero-span test covers this distinction. No musical notes are retimed.

Retry `42ae7cb0-77ea-44a8-965f-b8c4a9f0c539` completed the controlled source-plan
comparison. Retry `302d3eba-802a-4dce-be1d-1e242bf64834`, **Rest After the Stone**,
completed the fresh work with 321 direct-AI notes and eight populated parts. The
retry retained its own newly accepted macro/cast rather than requesting it again.
All six planned pitched joint owners were present in its complete-dialogue MIDI,
which was preserved exactly in the final score. However, a secondary violin not
selected as joint was authored afterward, adding further independent movement.
Neither result was presented as an accepted musical improvement.

### Complete pitched-ensemble variant

The next isolated option, `PULSO_AI_JOINT_PITCHED_SCORE_EXPERIMENT`, requires phrase
prosody and routes ALL available pitched cast members through complete ensemble
writing. The planner still chooses the foreground, rhythmic identity, supports and
relationships. This is a scheduling change, not a requirement for every instrument
to sound, a note quota or an enforced homorhythm. The AI writes chords, bass, inner
parts and melodic lines in the same phase; no later pitched lane can fill its gaps
without being part of that phase. Only unpitched percussion may remain afterward.
An all-pitched work needs no further accompaniment API calls.

The thematic-reference schema now also expresses the positive durations and MIDI
velocities already required by its parser. This prevents a zero-duration,
zero-velocity placeholder from satisfying the API schema and failing locally.
It is a representation correction, not a musical quality threshold.

## Completed whole-score evidence

The three-minute counterpart `9f8c8a61-8e1e-4ab6-aca0-c250c30672ca` completed:
974 notes, 12 populated parts, 263.6 seconds wall time. The accepted complete
pitched checkpoint is note-for-note identical to the same tracks in the final
MIDI; later percussion did not alter it. No within-track/channel same-key overlap
was found. The diagnostic clarinet entrance now begins at beat 67.5 and develops
predominantly on eighth-based supports, while the moving bass has longer values
at beats 64, 66, 67, 68, 70 and onward. The opening and climactic region retain
independent lines and some sixteenth coordinates: this is not forced homorhythm.
These observations are not proof that the listener will prefer the new passage.

The one-minute counterpart `ab31998a-6992-4d67-9c77-1304bbd2ada4` completed:
342 direct-AI notes, eight populated pitched parts, 72 seconds wall time. Every
pitched part was authored in the complete ensemble phase and preserved exactly.
Its secondary violin has 22 notes versus 53 in the earlier subset-written draft;
the piano has 105 versus 37. Those are AI decisions, not a thinning or filling
algorithm. The actual protagonist restores the opening subject in the final
section and reaches D at beat 116 following A7 at 112. Its final accompaniment
includes C and E as part of the explicitly planned Dm9, not accidental out-of-key
correction targets. One same-key cello reattack overlaps its previous D by 0.5
beat at beat 10. This is recorded as a reservation, not silently shortened or
ignored. Neither a valid cadence nor an overlap flag establishes artistic quality.

Both paired works retain an accepted plan; they do not by themselves prove transfer
to newly planned compositions. Fresh job `7d4adbc3-4fb3-46f3-840b-bba682168975`
therefore starts from the user-style prompt with a new seed and no imported macro,
phrase plan or MIDI. Its result must be inspected independently.

The read-only audit now records same-track/key/channel overlaps as advisory
evidence. It does not reject drafts, rewrite durations or classify all cross-voice
unisons as errors. Audits never claim that symbolic inspection is actual listening.

### Fresh transfer and optional whole-ensemble rehearsal

Fresh `7d4adbc3-4fb3-46f3-840b-bba682168975`, **The Descent Answered**, completed
with 321 direct-AI notes in eight pitched parts (276.98 seconds including its new
macro, cast and phrase plan). No imported musical plan or performance was used.
Its protagonist states A4-C5-B4-A4, expands/transposes its contour in development,
arrives at D5 after the climactic dominant, and returns to the initial subject
before the closing D4. All sounding activity ends at beat 116, leaving two seconds
of terminal rest. No hanging notes or within-track/key/channel overlap was found.
However, inner voices anticipate the dominant before the piano's declared change;
this remains a musical reservation, not a reason to advertise an approved result.

The existing optional AI rehearsal was therefore tested with ALL pitched owners,
not only foreground. Launch it explicitly:

```powershell
$env:PULSO_LOCAL_PORT='4202'
node web/local-studio/start-phrase-prosody.mjs --rehearsal
```

phrase plan but regenerates its actual score, then rehearses it once. It is NOT
an exact-note A/B against job `7d4...`. Its own first score is preserved in
`partial-01.mid`; its own selected revision is in `partial-02.mid` and the final

It completed in 112.72 seconds, with 307 direct-AI notes. The AI changed nine

dominant entrance at beat 56, so that improvement must NOT be attributed to the
later rehearsal. The review instead changed B-natural motif notes to Bb and

audition option, not a demonstrated universal improvement or the lab default.
No source-specific feedback, prescribed pitches or bar-addressed editing rules
were inserted in the generator for either run.

Candidate listening map (job `56f...`, 120 BPM):

- 0-8 s: complete opening proposition, not a collection of short attacks.
- 16-24 s: its continuation and the answering violin's related contour.
- 30-46 s: compressed subject and sustained dominant climax, then the D arrival.
- 52-60 s: recognizable return, final descending D and two-second terminal rest.

Residual reservations remain: some answering entries are deliberately staggered;
two held piano common tones are reattacked at beat 80 before their earlier releases.
These are documented for listening rather than automatically clipped or turned
into a prohibition on counterpoint. The legacy scalar production audit is not
treated as an artistic verdict (it also applies electronic-layer checks to this
chamber score). No candidate is a production anchor without listener acceptance.

The architectural result is reusable coauthorship of the pitched ensemble with an
explicit phrase prosody and an optional actual-score AI rehearsal. The existing
accepted production profile remains untouched. Repeating a seed does not promise
the same MIDI, and successful symbolic analysis cannot promise human preference.
