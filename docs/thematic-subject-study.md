The user rejected the musical coherence of **Bearing Stone, Returning**
(`4405195e-306d-40bc-9cea-c55ed58a01bb`). Its valid MIDI, tonal centre,

The next hypothesis concerns recognition of complete thoughts rather than

continual replacement of the very material a listener needs to recognize.
The old plan also refers to what earlier voices said before those voices
have actually been composed.

## Experiment

`PULSO_AI_THEMATIC_SUBJECT_EXPERIMENT=1` asks the AI phrase planner to author
a complete `musical_subject` reference, with its own local note coordinates,
owner, audible identity, continuation, arrival and recognizable-return
intention. The normal phrase plan remains. The subject is not a new set
of compulsory global MIDI timestamps and is never inserted into the score.

The complete-dialogue writer receives the actual reference notes. It authors
all definitive notes itself, including any literal recurrence or variation.
No code copies the reference, transposes it, creates rhythmic variants,
adds an accompaniment, removes notes or changes pitches. The experiment
permits recognizable full statements and stable pulse alongside contrast;
it does not impose a song form, phrase length, genre template or cadence
frequency. Representation bounds and MIDI safety remain technical checks,
not a new musical scoring gate.

Tests cover disabled-profile isolation, valid/invalid reference representation,
and the absence of renderer-generated MIDI from a reference-only plan.
The read-only thematic audit reports actual contiguous reference-prefix
matches in pitch, onset and sounding duration, with possible transposition
or rhythmic scaling. It never applies such transformations. Missing matches
do not prove an absence of development, and matching does not prove quality.
