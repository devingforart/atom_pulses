# Secondary arrangement experiment

Base: 0e20ab1 (Dorian Current). Local v1.2 only; not deployed.

Preserve foreground spaciousness while asking the AI to choose independent
secondary voices and meaningful interventions at cast, plan and writer stages.
Extend existing ensemble_parts with relationship and occurrence: thematic
connection, location, destination and whether a gesture returns or happens once.
The existing arc-local note plans, technical validation and window translation
carry the concrete notes. Legacy plans without these descriptions remain valid.

Options include developed countermelody, tonal fills, connecting passages,
anticipations, evolving textures and tension/release. They are not compulsory
tracks or scheduled fills. No procedural note generation, copying, density
filling, retiming or pitch correction is introduced. Synth sweeps are explicitly
distinguished from what a neutral-piano MIDI preview can demonstrate.

Test a fresh three-minute Club composition with the unchanged prompt
`musica como la de hernan cattaneo`, 122 BPM, strict scale and first pass.
Do not reuse blueprint, MIDI or seed. Evaluate actual secondary development,
entry preparation, preserved groove and foreground clarity after completion;
schema compliance alone does not demonstrate musical improvement.
